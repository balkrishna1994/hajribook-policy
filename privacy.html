Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 20, 2026

Welcome to HajriBook ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you log in via Google Sign-In or email, we collect your email address and basic profile details.
  • Business & Profile Data: Information you voluntarily provide, such as your Firm Name, Owner Name, Address, GST, and PAN numbers.
  • Staff & Attendance Data: Information regarding staff members, laborers, wages, attendance records, and cashbook transactions entered into the app.
  • Device Contacts (Optional): If you choose to add staff directly from your phonebook, we access your contacts solely for that purpose with your permission.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the HajriBook app features (attendance tracking, cashbook, and PDF report generation).
  • To secure your account and manage local app locks.
  • To backup and sync your business data securely if enabled.

3. Data Security

Your core attendance and financial data are primarily stored locally on your device. We implement security measures, including local PIN/Biometric locking options, to protect your business records from unauthorized access.

4. Third-Party Services

We utilize trusted third-party services such as Firebase Authentication for secure login. These services have their own privacy policies governing data handling.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
